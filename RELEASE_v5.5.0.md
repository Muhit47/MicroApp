## Installing

Three downloads: MicroApp-5.5.0-setup.exe (all users, Program Files), MicroApp-5.5.0-peruser-setup.exe (just you, no admin), or the portable MicroApp-5.5.0-win-x64.zip. Each is about 165 MB because the offline background-removal model is included. Details and silent switches are in [SETUP.md](https://github.com/Mahi-BD/MicroApp/blob/main/SETUP.md). Installing over an older version keeps your settings and notes.
